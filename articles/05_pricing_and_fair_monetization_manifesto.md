# The FrankDiary Monetization Manifesto: Honest Pricing vs Predatory Subscriptions

How did we get to a point where a personal diary app charges $60 to $80 every single year just to store text files?

If you examine the tech industry in 2026, venture-capital-backed software companies face intense pressure to deliver infinite recurring revenue. To satisfy investors, apps that used to cost $5 as a one-time purchase have transitioned into aggressive, recurring subscription treadmills.

When a user stops paying their $60/year subscription to Day One or Journey, their access is often degraded, their sync is cut off, and their personal memories are gated.

At **FrankBase**, founder **Master Manikant Yadav** rejected this model entirely. Here is our ethical monetization manifesto for **[FrankDiary](https://dairy.frankbase.com)**.

---

## The 3 Ethical Principles of FrankDiary Monetization

### Principle 1: Private Writing is a Fundamental Human Right
A person should never be forced to pay a recurring monthly fee simply to document their life, reflect on their mental health, or confess their feelings.  
**Therefore: The FrankDiary Offline Core Vault is 100% Free Forever.**

### Principle 2: Value-Based Cloud Pricing, Not Extortion
Cloud infrastructure costs money. Storing encrypted database records on Cloudflare D1 and global edge servers incurs real costs. But those costs are a few cents per gigabyte per year—not $70 per user.  
**Therefore: We price our cloud sync at fair, transparent, and accessible rates.**

### Principle 3: Zero Vendor Lock-in & Full Data Custody
You should stay with FrankDiary because you love the software, not because your memories are held hostage.  
**Therefore: You can export all your entries in open JSON or authenticated `.fbe` format with 1 click at any time, completely free.**
