## 2. Market Sizing Calculations (TAM, SAM, SOM)

### Total Addressable Market (TAM): \$5.2 Billion
* The global digital journaling app market was valued at **\$1.8 Billion in 2024** and is projected to reach **\$3.5 Billion by 2030** (CAGR of 11.2%).
* The consumer privacy and personal encryption software market represents an additional **\$1.7 Billion** (VPNs, password managers, secure notes).
* **Total Global TAM:** **\$5.2 Billion**.

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): \$480 Million
* Focuses on smartphone and desktop users actively searching for privacy-first, secure, or offline journaling alternatives (disillusioned with Day One's subscription, Apple Journal's lock-in, or Evernote's surveillance).
* Target geographies: North America, Europe, India, and East Asia (English and Hindi interfaces).
* Estimated at ~9.2% of the global TAM: **\$480 Million**.

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — 3-Year Horizon: \$3.8 Million
* Capturing 0.8% of the SAM within 36 months by leveraging zero-ad organic growth, open-source credibility, and cross-promotion across Master Manikant Yadav's ecosystem.
* Translates to roughly **120,000 active free users** and **10,000 paying Pro subscribers** generating ~\$300,000+ in annual recurring revenue (ARR).

## 3. Core Target Customer Personas

### Persona A: "The Vulnerable Journaler" (Ananya, 26, Bangalore)
* **Background:** Software designer, writes daily reflections on relationships, imposter syndrome, and therapy exercises.
* **Pain Point:** Terrified that a cloud leak, hacker, or curious family member might read her raw diary entries. Uncomfortable storing unencrypted feelings on Google Keep or Apple Notes.
* **FrankDiary Fit:** Values the local PIN/biometric app lock, 100% offline isolation, and complete peace of mind that no server admin can ever read her thoughts.

### Persona B: "The Privacy Maximalist & Developer" (Marcus, 34, Berlin)
* **Background:** Systems engineer, runs Linux, uses Signal and Bitwarden, refuses closed-source proprietary software.
* **Pain Point:** Abandoned Skiff when Notion shut it down. Dislikes Obsidian's closed-source core and complex sync setup.
* **FrankDiary Fit:** Loves the open-source GitHub codebase, verified Argon2id parameters, reproducible builds, and the standalone `.fbe` container format.

### Persona C: "The Strategic Founder & Creative" (Rajiv, 41, Delhi/Mumbai)
* **Background:** Startup founder and angel investor, journals daily business ideas, negotiation notes, and confidential strategy memos.
* **Pain Point:** Cannot risk trade secrets or sensitive negotiations being scraped for AI training or subpoenaed in vague third-party legal requests.
* **FrankDiary Fit:** Leverages Mode 3 (Pro Sync) across MacBook and iPhone with zero corporate surveillance risk.
